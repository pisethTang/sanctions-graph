1. Do not say "this matters - `<text>` because ___" pattern
2. Do not use em-dashes.
3. Do not say "this is the wrong shape ..." pattern
4. Do not use the "it is not because of this __ but it's because of that ___" pattern
5. Do not say "not a `<noun>` (e.g. not a 'hack')"
6. That's the difference between `<a>` and `<b>`. E.g. The cold start goes from a minute to under a second. That's the difference between a portfolio link that looks broken and one that just feels slightly slow.
7. Don't say "The hard part ___, it's ___." E.g., The hard part isn't finding the answer. It's getting quiet enough to hear it.
8. Don't say "`<Idea a>`, **not** `<idea b>`" (especially the word **not** shouldn't be there and the binary distinction must not be present.) E.g., Two Vercel projects, not one.
9. Don't say something along the line of "... and it's the part most likely to bite."
10. Don't say the word "genuine".
